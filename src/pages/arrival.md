# Roles

## Host

The host player acts as both the character hosting the gala and the referee for
the game as a whole.

## Guest

# Aspirations

Each player draws three aspiration cards, keeps one, and discards the rest.
Aspiration cards are there for guidance and as an opportunity for additional
points.

# Stats

<figure class="epigraph">
<blockquote><p>Pride relates more to our opinion of ourselves; vanity to what we would have others think of us.</p></blockquote>
<figcaption>— Mary Bennet, <cite>Pride and Prejudice</cite>, ch. 5</figcaption>
</figure>

Stats range from 0&ndash;10. They represent what others perceive about a
player's character, rather than what that character actually is.

All players will begin the game with a total of 25 points to distribute amongst
their stats as they wish.

## Wealth

This stat is unique among the others in that it may be exchanged between

## Accomplishment

Determines the amount of skills a character is perceived to be good at. Upon
gaining or losing Accomplishment points, a player must adjust their character's
skills to reflect their total Accomplishment.

# Reputation

<figure class="epigraph">
<blockquote><p>My good opinion once lost is lost for ever.</p></blockquote>
<figcaption>— Mr. Darcy, <cite>Pride and Prejudice</cite>, ch. 11</figcaption>
</figure>

A character's reputation is the sum total of all of their stats. When a player
gains or loses reputation points, they may add or subtract that number divided
amongst any of their stats. A stat may not fall below zero or go above 10.

The player with the highest reputation at the end of the night wins the game.

# Secrets

Each player writes five secrets about their character, of varying severity. If a
secret would contain details pertaining to something other than the character
themselves (ie. implicating involvement with another character), the player
leaves those details as a blank. Once a player has written their secrets, they
give them to the host.

Once the host has received secrets from each player, they will do the following:

- Grade the severity of each secret and score it from 1 (trivial) to 5
  (egregious). The score is written in the top right hand corner of the card.
- Fill in any blanks. The game will be at its most entertaining when players'
  secrets intertwine with each other. The host may include the host character's
  backstory and details.
- Assign additional secrets to fill in the blanks. If a player might partially
  know another player's secret due to involvement, the host gives them a
  redacted copy of the other player's secret. Any additional secrets should be
  scored.

Once secrets have been returned to their players, the table takes a moment to
pause. The host makes sure each player is fully comfortable with the contents of
their updated secrets before the game begins.
