A house already furnished: five discoveries to a room, for the Host to place
before the evening begins. Use them as they stand, alter them freely, or put
something of your own in their stead.

Whatever is found, the Host writes upon a slip and hands over discreetly. Once
taken, a discovery is gone, and the room holds it no longer.

# The Conservatory

- **Two Voices Behind the Palms** — The Host hands you a redacted copy of a
  secret concerning one of the speakers.
- **A Dropped Glove** — Embroidered with initials that are not its owner's.
  Advantage on your next attack against the guest the Host names.
- **A Pressed Flower** — Kept far longer than any friendship would explain.
  Worth three coin to the guest it concerns, should you care to ask them for it.
- **A Bench Still Warm** — Somebody left in a hurry. You learn which guest was
  not where they claimed to be.
- **Nothing But the Orchids** — A quarter hour of peace, and one coin you had
  forgotten was in your pocket.

# The Library

- **An Unfinished Letter** — The Host hands you a redacted copy of a secret
  concerning its writer.
- **A Ledger Left Open** — Somebody's accounts do not balance. Advantage on your
  next attack against the guest the Host names.
- **A Novel with a Name Inside** — Lent once, and never returned. Worth two coin
  to its owner.
- **A Locked Drawer** — You may force it and take what is inside, but any guest
  who sees you do it may call Faux Pas.
- **Dust and Sermons** — Nothing worth having. But nobody saw you either, and
  your next exploration is made with advantage.

# The Terrace

- **A Torn Invitation** — The Host hands you a redacted copy of a secret
  concerning the guest whose name is on it.
- **A Cigar Still Burning** — Somebody stepped out alone, and you learn who.
- **Coins in the Fountain** — Take two coin, and think no more about whose
  wishes they were.
- **The Long View of the Ballroom** — You have seen exactly how the room stands.
  Advantage on your next retort.
- **Only the Cold** — Nothing at all, though the air does you good. Advantage on
  your next Charm roll.

# The Card Room

- **A Whispered Wager** — The Host hands you a redacted copy of a secret
  concerning one of the players.
- **A Debt Written on a Napkin** — Somebody owes somebody rather more than they
  can pay. Advantage on your next attack against the guest the Host names.
- **A Winning Hand Abandoned** — Take three coin from the table. Nobody is
  looking for it.
- **A Marked Deck** — Proof of something thoroughly ungentlemanly. Worth four
  coin to the guest it belongs to.
- **The Company of Gamblers** — You are seen to be entirely at your ease.
  Advantage on your next Charm roll.
