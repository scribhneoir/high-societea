The guests are not the only things of intrest lying withing a grand estate.
An unengaged guest may choose to spend their action to search a room in the estate
by making a check against their wit. A successful check will result in a Discovery.
The Host may choose to use the table below or substitute with something of their choosing.
The higher the roll, the rarer the find.

Whatever is found, the Host writes upon a slip and hands over discreetly.
