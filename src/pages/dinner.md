The final three rounds before the evening's conclusion are spent over dinner.
There are no rules of engagement and everyone is in earshot of everyone else.
Players use this time to tie up any loose threads.

# The Night

Each player adds up their current reputation and points from aspiration cards.
The player with the greatest reputation is the evening's winner!
