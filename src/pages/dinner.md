The final three rounds before the evening's conclusion are spent over
dinner. There are no rules of engagement and everyone is in earshot of everyone
else. No conversation is private, and a character need spend no engagement to
address whomever they please.

**In every other respect the rules of the gala hold.** Turns pass clockwise as
before, and a character may attack, retort, call Faux Pas, sell a secret, or
give way to an outburst just as they did upon the floor. Players use this time
to tie up any loose threads.

# The Night

Each player adds up their current reputation — the sum of their stats together
with whatever coin remains in their purse — and the points from their
aspiration card. The player with the greatest reputation is the evening's
winner!

Should two guests finish level, the victory falls to whichever has **the fewest
secrets still held against them**: count the cards concerning their character
that remain in the hands of others. If they are level in that too, they must
share the evening between them, which neither will enjoy in the slightest.
