## Worth Two

- **A Turn About the Room** — Dance at least once, with anybody at all.
- **Seen and Admired** — Be asked to dance by another guest.
- **The Confidante** — Be sold a secret by two different guests.
- **A Full Purse** — Receive coin from another guest, as a gift or as a bribe.
- **A Modest Fortune** — Finish the evening with more coin than you began with.
- **The Sharpened Tongue** — Land a successful retort.

## Worth Three

- **Thick as Thieves** — Dance twice with the same partner.
- **The Whisper Campaign** — Sell the same secret to three different guests.
- **Unimpeachable** — Have Faux Pas called against you, and lose nothing by it.
- **A Debt Repaid** — Give a guest coin, and later cut that same guest down.
- **The Interruption** — Tear a guest away from another's conversation.
- **The Honest Broker** — Sell a secret, having never once crafted a rumor.

## Worth Four

- **The Ruin of Another** — Reduce one of another guest's stats to zero.
- **Beneath Notice** — Reach the dinner without once being attacked.
- **The Collector** — Hold, at the tally, a secret concerning every other guest.
- **The Forger** — Sell a rumor of your own invention that is never accused.
- **The Vulture** — Gain reputation by the sale of secrets three times over.
- **Above Reproach** — Reach the dinner with your Charm never having fallen.

## Worth Five

- **Untouched** — Finish with no secret of yours in any other guest's hand.
- **The Duel** — Win an exchange of retorts running three deep or more.
- **The Toast of the Evening** — Finish with every one of your stats at five or
  above.
- **The Architect** — Bring two different guests to an outburst.
- **The Spider** — Sell secrets concerning three different guests.
- **Ruined and Radiant** — Have a secret of yours revealed to the room, and
  finish the night with a greater reputation than you began it with.
