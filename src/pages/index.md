A guest would best keep their secrets close to their chest. For amidst a gala of
flowing gowns and mirthful laughter, silver tongues and darting eyes search to
feast on the weak.

High Societea is a one shot TTRPG in which players embody scandalous characters,
using catty banter and scrupulous wit to pry secrets from one another until all
is laid bare.

# The Table

High Societea is played by five to eight people, one of whom takes the role
of the Host. An evening lasts some two to three hours.

The table will need a pair of modified D6 dice, a deck of aspiration cards, a
supply of index cards on which to write secrets and discoveries, and a quantity of 
coin for the guests to carry, bestow, and be bought with.

# The Evening

## [Chapter One • The Arrival](/arrival)

Roles, aspirations, stats and reputation, the persona each player wears, the
safety conversation, and the five secrets every guest hands to the Host.

## [Chapter Two • The Gala](/gala)

The party, the rounds and their dances, and every way a guest may cut another
down: attacks and retorts, faux pas, sold secrets, crafted rumors, and
outbursts.

## [Chapter Three • The Dinner](/dinner)

The last three rounds, loose threads tied, and the final tally that names the
evening's winner.

## [Appendix • The Aspiration Deck](/aspirations)

Four and twenty aspirations to draw from, from a turn about the room to the ruin
of another guest.

## [Appendix • Discoveries](/discoveries)

A house already furnished, with things to be found in the conservatory, the
library, the terrace, and the card room.
