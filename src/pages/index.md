A guest would best keep their secrets close to their chest. For amidst a gala of
flowing gowns and mirthful laughter, silver tongues and darting eyes search to
feast on the weak.

High Societea is a one shot TTRPG in which players embody scandalous characters,
using catty banter and scrupulous wit to pry secrets from one another until all
is laid bare.

> High Societea is played using modified D6 dice. All sixes are considered
> zeros, with the values of a die ranging from zero to five.

# The Evening

## Chapter One • The Arrival

**[The Arrival](/arrival)** — roles, aspirations, stats and reputation, the persona
each player wears, the safety conversation, and the five secrets every guest
hands to the Host.

## Chapter Two • The Gala

**[The Gala](/gala)** — the party, the rounds and their dances, and every way
a guest may cut another down: attacks and retorts, faux pas, sold secrets,
crafted rumors, and outbursts.

## Chapter Three • The Dinner

**[The Dinner](/dinner)** — the last three rounds, loose threads tied, and the
final tally that names the evening's winner.
